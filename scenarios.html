Production & Delivery Responsibilities

Focus on what you actually own and contribute: production support, issue analysis, releases, validation, communication, code review and engineering processes.

Your production responsibility

Production support

Review logs, New Relic/monitoring, database and system signals to understand affected areas and support technical troubleshooting.

Release support

Review release impact, changed modules/files, testing requirements, dependencies and ownership before production.

Validation

Support smoke checks and compare expected behavior/monitoring after a fix or deployment.

Coordination

Share impact, evidence, testing needs and next actions with developers, QC and leads.

Speakable answer

“My production responsibility is mainly technical support and release ownership. I help analyze production issues using logs, monitoring, database and system-level signals, coordinate with developers and QC on reproduction and validation, support fixes and deployments, and verify the affected flow after the change. I keep the explanation high level unless deeper technical detail is needed.”

Code review responsibility

Before commit

Changed Magento modules go through local quality checks such as PHPCS and unit-test validation.

During review

Manual review is supported by Cursor AI using project/Magento rules. Human reviewers make the final decision.

Before production

Release review looks at the complete diff, module impact, testing scenarios, file ownership and ticket mapping.

Likely CTO questions

What is your responsibility during a production issue?

I help understand impact, review technical evidence, narrow the affected area, support the fix/release process, coordinate validation and verify behavior afterward. I do not need to present myself as the formal owner of the RCA.

What do you check before a production release?

Changed modules/files, dependencies, testing scenarios, ownership, high-risk areas, mandatory quality gates and whether affected business flows have enough validation.

How do you help QC when an issue is difficult to reproduce?

Share useful logs, identifiers, user-flow information and technical conditions so QC and developers can reproduce the same behavior instead of repeating generic attempts.

How do you reduce release risk?

Early code-quality checks, manual review, complete release-diff visibility, module-impact analysis, ownership/ticket mapping, regression scope and post-deployment verification.

What engineering process have you improved?

The release-review workflow is the strongest example: it turns a manual activity into a repeatable process with structured impact/testing output and file-level traceability.
